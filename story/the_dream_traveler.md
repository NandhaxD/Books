# 🌙 THE DREAM TRAVELER  

**Written by:** NandhaXD  

---

## 🎬 Logline  
A reclusive mathematician discovers he can rewrite his past through his dreams, only to find that every "correction" comes with a terrifying price and a reality he no longer recognizes.  

---

## I. The Awakening  
- **Sung**, a brilliant but isolated freelance mathematician and software developer, works tirelessly from home.  
- Exhausted by logic and code, he falls into a deep sleep.  
- In the void, an **Unpredictable Face**—a shifting, shadowy entity—appears and whispers:  
  > *“You are the chosen one.”*  
- When Sung wakes, life seems normal, but the line between reality and sleep begins to blur.  
- He suddenly passes out and finds himself reliving moments from his past.  

---

## II. The First Travels  

### 🖤 The Memory of Her  
- Sung finds himself in a past conversation with his ex-girlfriend.  
- To test its reality, he touches her hand—the warmth is undeniable.  
- She looks at him, confused, as he speaks differently than before.  
- Suddenly, he wakes on a bench in the present, struck by his mother.  
- His remote job has changed—his company now requires him to work in a physical office.  

### 🏢 The Office Suspension  
- At his new office, Sung falls into another trance.  
- He is transported back to his previous job, reliving the moment his manager handed him a suspension letter.  
- Frozen, he looks haunted. His manager mocks him:  
  > *“Why are you stuck? Seen a ghost? Talk!”*  
- In reality, his current supervisor yells at him for sleeping on the job.  

---

## III. The Butterfly Effect  
- Sung meets his ex-girlfriend at a hotel.  
- She shocks him by remembering the **new past** he created.  
- Realizing his power, Sung travels back to his university years.  
- He retakes his entrance exams in his dream, using future knowledge to achieve top marks.  
- He wakes in a new timeline:  
  - A prestigious, high-paying job.  
  - A successful life.  
- But he discovers he is married to someone else—his academic success altered the course of love and destiny.  

---

## IV. The Nightmare Reality  
- The more Sung travels, the more terrifying his dreams become.  
- People inside his dreams begin to act strangely, breaking the script of the past.  
- They look directly at him, aware he is an intruder.  
- Some even try to kill him within the dream to prevent him from waking.  
- Sung confronts the shadowy **God-creature** from the beginning.  
- He asks why he was given this power.  
  - The creature answers:  
    > *“To let you fulfill your dreams. But even dreams have bad events. Perfection is a lie.”*  

---

## V. Conclusion  
- Exhausted and terrified of the distorted reality he created, Sung begs the creature to take the power back.  
- He realizes that a life without mistakes isn’t truly life.  
- Through a final mental battle in his subconscious, Sung rejects the easy fixes of the **Dream Traveler**.  
- He wakes in his original, messy reality.  
- Determined, he chooses to fix his life through hard work and genuine human connection.  
- At last, he finds peace.
